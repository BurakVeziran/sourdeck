# SourDeck Support

Report issues and share suggestions through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/BurakVeziran/sourdeck/issues). Developer: Burak Veziran.

## Reporting an issue

- Include your SourDeck version and operating system version.
- Describe the steps that led to the issue, what you expected to happen, and any error message you saw.
- If helpful, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden.

GitHub Issues may be public. Do not share passwords, email addresses, session files, encryption keys, private messages, personal notes, or screenshots containing account information. Do not upload your full database or export file. For privacy questions, start with a description that does not contain personal data.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use SourDeck without adding an account?

Yes. You can read public content in anonymous mode. Publishing entries, messaging, and other account actions require signing in with your Ekşi Sözlük account. Your account's permissions on the platform apply.

### Does live following work when the app is closed?

No. Live following and local notifications work while SourDeck is running. An internet connection and access to the Ekşi Sözlük service are required.

### Where is my session stored?

Session information is stored encrypted on this device. The encryption key is also on the device; anyone who can access both the session files and the key may be able to use the session. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

### Does removing an account from the app delete my Ekşi Sözlük account?

No. Signing out of the app or removing a saved account does not delete your Ekşi Sözlük account. Whether local drafts and collections are deleted depends on your choices in the account removal screen. Use Ekşi Sözlük's own account management tools to manage your Ekşi Sözlük account and content published on the platform.

### Can I move my collections to another device?

You can select and export workspaces and collections from the data transfer screen. The file may contain personal notes and tags, so keep it in a safe place. Session information and private messages are not included in the transfer. You will need to sign in again on the other device.

### What should I do if content does not load?

Check your internet connection and the app's connection status. If the server has specified a waiting period, wait for it to expire. Account-specific actions may require signing in again. Report persistent issues along with the error text.

### Where should I report inappropriate content?

Report content published on Ekşi Sözlük to the service through the platform's own reporting channels. You can report issues with SourDeck's display or filtering behavior through GitHub Issues.

SourDeck is not the official Ekşi Sözlük app.
